1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app "AT Führerschein-B Quiz TR/DE" (package name: sep_2026.fs_quiz_de_tr), published on Google Play by the developer SA&SM. This app is an independent, unofficial learning aid designed to help Turkish-speaking residents in Austria study for and pass the official Austrian driving licence exam (Führerscheinprüfung). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any Austrian government authority or official examination body. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

2. Data We Collect

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All quiz progress and results are stored locally on your device only.

3. No Account Required

This app does not require you to create an account or log in.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
