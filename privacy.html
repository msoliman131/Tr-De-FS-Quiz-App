1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app "AT Führerschein-B Quiz TR/DE" (package name: juni_2026.fs_quiz_de_ar), published on Google Play by the developer SA&SM. This app is an independent, unofficial learning aid designed to help Arabic-speaking residents in Austria study for and pass the official Austrian driving licence exam (Führerscheinprüfung). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any Austrian government authority or official examination body. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

2. Data We Collect

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All quiz progress and results are stored locally on your device only.

3. No Account Required

This app does not require you to create an account or log in.

4. In-App Purchases

This app offers an optional 1-year subscription to unlock full content. Purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing and managed using RevenueCat, a third-party purchase management service. RevenueCat may process anonymized purchase and device identifiers (such as an anonymous app user ID) solely to validate purchases, manage entitlements, and handle subscription renewals. No personally identifiable information is shared with RevenueCat beyond what is required for purchase and subscription validation. For more information, see RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Third-Party Services

Aside from Google Play Billing and RevenueCat (used only for processing in-app purchases and subscriptions), this app does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services at this time.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
